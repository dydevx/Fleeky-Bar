# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Design-conscious customers in Amsterdam looking for professional nail care, distinctive nail art, and a straightforward way to review prices and book an appointment.

## Product Purpose

Present Fleeky Bar as a distinctive premium nail studio, make treatments and prices easy to understand, and convert visitors into online bookings or visits to the Amsterdam studio.

## Brand Personality

Bold, sensual, fashion-forward. The experience should feel confident, artistic, high-end, and slightly nocturnal without losing clarity or professionalism.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic nail salon styling: white or beige surfaces, cute pastel pink, soft feminine minimalism, generic beauty templates, cluttered chrome decoration, and layouts that obscure practical information.

## Design Principles

- Let real nail work prove the studio's craft.
- Use chrome as a precise accent, not visual noise.
- Keep every booking path obvious and consistent.
- Balance strong fashion-editorial expression with effortless scanning.
- Preserve the brand's dark plum identity on every viewport.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, readable contrast, meaningful alt text, and responsive layouts optimized for 375px, 390px, and 430px mobile widths.
